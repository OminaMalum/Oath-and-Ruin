PactFall, formerly Oath & Ruin · naming and title treatment

Naming report

Searches run 20 October 2026, three rounds. Preliminary research, not legal clearance.

Working name, picked by Anthony
PACTFALL

An English coinage: the moment the pacts break. Said PAKT-fall. No game on Steam or itch.io, no web result and no US trademark (exact or PACT*FALL) turned up. pactfall.com is registered by someone else; playpactfall.com and pactfallgame.com were unregistered on 20 October.

Before it becomes permanent: a trademark attorney should run a full clearance search (US first, then EU and WIPO, which I could not reach from here).

How we got here

Round 1 (below) recommended Kivivala, which turned out hard to say. Rounds 2 and 3 went for short, punchy names that are easy to say aloud. Same checks each time: Steam, itch.io, web, USPTO, .com and YouTube handle.

Round 1 · Why move off "Oath & Ruin"

The exact phrase is not a registered US mark and no game uses it, but the field around it is crowded with games:

  • OATH is a registered US mark in board games (Leder Games, Oath: Chronicles of Empire and Exile, class 28), a strategy game about kingdoms falling to ruin.
  • OATH and OATH GAMING are registered in entertainment services for esports (Oath Gaming LLC, class 41).
  • OATHSWORN (board game, class 28) is registered; ASHEN OATH and OATHFALL are pending in class 28.
  • Oathborn: The Chronicle of Ruin is a fantasy tactics game on Steam, and Oathborne also lists there.
  • The YouTube handle @oathandruin belongs to a channel named "Oath & Ruin" (organized crime history).

A short generic phrase made of two common words is also hard to find in search and weak as a trademark. oathandruin.com is unregistered, but that does not make the name safe.

Round 1 · Finalists

Round 1 pick, set aside

KIVIVALA

Meaning
"Stone oath". Kivi (stone, Kotus type 7) and vala (oath, type 9) are both everyday Finnish words. Finnish freely builds compounds this way (kivitalo, stone house), but kivivala is our coinage, not a dictionary word.
Pronunciation
English: KIV-ee-vah-lah. Finnish: /ˈkiʋiˌʋɑlɑ/, stress on the first syllable.
Fit
Oaths cut into weathered stone, rival orders bound by them, and what happens when they break. It suits the carved stone look and leaves room for a stone objective in the arena.
Weaknesses
Means nothing to English players without a tagline. Possible misspellings (Kivivalla, Kiviwala). Vala is also the imperative of valaa "pour, cast", so a Finn might read "stone, cast!": needs a native read. Unrelated Finnish sign maker Kivivalo Oy is one letter away.
Latin

CONIURATI

Meaning
Authentic Latin: "conspirators", literally those sworn together (Cicero and Sallust use coniūrātiō for Catiline's plot).
Pronunciation
English: kon-yoo-RAH-tee. Classical: [kɔn.juːˈraː.tiː].
Fit
Each team is a sworn band of three. The "conjure" echo suits a spell-heavy game.
Weaknesses
Five syllables. People will type "Conjurati". The real meaning leans villainous. Conjurers (Steam, 2026) and many CONJURE marks sit nearby.
Original English

VOWFALL

Meaning
Our coinage: the moment a vow falls. Carries "oath and ruin" in one word.
Pronunciation
VOW-fall. The easiest of the three to say, spell and recommend aloud.
Fit
Direct and readable, in the same register as other fantasy game names.
Weaknesses
One sound away from VOIDFALL, a registered board game mark (class 28). vowfall.com is registered. A YouTube channel uses "VOWFALL Stories". The "-fall" pattern is common, so it is less distinctive.

Gaps in this search

  • WIPO Global Brand Database is behind a captcha and TMview / EUIPO did not respond to the sandbox, so no international or EU trademark search was done. Run these before committing.
  • Finnish trademark register (PRH) not checked, which matters for a Finnish word.
  • Epic Games Store, GOG, console stores, app stores and BoardGameGeek were only covered through general web search.
  • X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Twitch and GitHub handles could not be checked (login walls or blocked). .gg and .game domains could not be checked.
  • USPTO results are what its public search returned that day. Brand new filings can take time to appear, and a search does not cover common law use.
  • No native Finnish or Latin speaker has reviewed any name. Sources were Wiktionary entries (with Kotus declension types and IPA) for vala, raunio, kivi, coniūrātiō and coniūrātus.

"No obvious conflict found" in this table means exactly that. It is not legal clearance, and an unregistered domain or empty store search does not mean a name is free to use.

Title treatments

Three concepts for PACTFALL plus the combination you picked, built in code from licensed fonts plus our own geometry. No image generation, nothing traced, nothing taken from the reference collage. The art below follows your light or dark theme.

2b · Broken seal with stele lettering (your pick)

The broken seal from concept 2 over the Forum capitals from concept 1, crossbarless A's included. The stone stays cut from Marcellus, whose heavier V holds up at icon sizes, so the standalone mark is unchanged.

1 · Carved stele

Forum capitals with our own spacing. Both A's are a mirrored V with no crossbar, as in early inscriptions, so P Λ C T F Λ L L carries two sharp peaks. Incised rules frame it, the lower one broken at the centre, with triangular word stops at the ends.

2 · Broken seal

A double ring cut open at one point, the broken oath, around a stone made from the name's own letter: two crossbarless A's point to point. Marcellus capitals below, widely spaced. The seal works alone as the icon.

3 · Manuscript colophon

Cormorant Garamond SemiBold in mixed case, tightened and evened. A hair rule below ends in lozenges, with a hannunvaakuna at its centre: the looped square, an old Finnish and Nordic protective sign, drawn from scratch. Shown as PactFall; the Pactfall version (3b) puts a small diamond between Pact and fall instead.

What I checked in the renders

  • Rendered every file in Chromium and looked at each one. No clipping: every PNG has ink-free margins on all four sides. No empty or malformed paths, and no missing glyphs (all letters exist in each font).
  • Editable masters render the same as the outlined files.
  • Small sizes: the stele and the seal lockup need 240 px of width. The seal mark holds at 32 px but fills in at 16 px. The colophon reads best small, down to about 120 px.
  • Monochrome versions hold up for all three, since no concept depends on color or gradients.
